What MindZone is

MindZone is a free mental-performance training app for athletes aged 10–18. It helps you practise things like focus, confidence, handling nerves, and bouncing back after a bad game.

What MindZone is not

MindZone is not therapy, counseling, or medical care, and it never replaces them. The MindZone Coach is an AI. It is not a doctor, therapist, counselor, or licensed professional of any kind, and it cannot diagnose or treat anything. Nothing in the app is medical advice.

AI can also simply be wrong. Use your own judgement, and check anything important with a real adult you trust — a parent, guardian, coach, teacher, or counselor.

If you need real help right now

Please talk to a trusted adult today. These are free and available any time:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line — text HOME to 741741
  • Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline — 1-800-422-4453
  • If someone is in danger right now, call 911.

Who can use it

  • You need to be between 10 and 18 years old.
  • A parent or guardian has to say it’s okay. We ask you to confirm this when you set up your profile.
  • One account per person. Keep your password to yourself.

Using it fairly

Please don’t:

  • Pretend to be someone else, or use someone else’s account.
  • Try to break, overload, or get around the limits in the app.
  • Use the coach chat to send abusive, hateful, or sexual messages.

We may pause or close an account that is being used this way.

Your stuff

What you write stays yours. You can clear your coach conversation whenever you want, and you can delete your account and everything attached to it. How we handle your information is explained in our Privacy Policy.
