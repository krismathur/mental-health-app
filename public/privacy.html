Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

MindZone is a mental-performance training app for athletes aged 10–18. Because our users are young, we collect as little as we can and we do not sell anything to anyone. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what we store and who else sees it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only as a bcrypt hash, so nobody at MindZone can read your actual password.
  • Your player profile: your first name, age, sport, your goal, your biggest challenge, how long you want your plan to be, and your self-ratings for confidence, stress, focus and bouncing back.
  • Your training plan: the plan generated for you and its progress.
  • Your coach chats: the messages you send to the MindZone Coach and the replies you get back.
  • Safety flags: if a message triggers our crisis screening, we record that it happened, which category it fell into, and when. We do not copy the message text into this record.
  • A login cookie so you stay signed in, and some settings saved in your own browser (your avatar and progress).

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or analytics trackers of any kind. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, nothing similar.
  • No location data, no contacts, no camera or microphone access.
  • No payment information — MindZone is free.

Who else sees your information

MindZone uses Google's Gemini API to write your training plan, power the coach chat, and generate the guided-audio voice. To do that, the following is sent to Google:

  • For your plan: your first name, age, sport, goal, challenge and your self-ratings.
  • For the coach: the message you type, plus roughly your last 20 messages so the coach remembers the conversation.
  • For guided audio: the text of the meditation script (this contains nothing about you).

Google processes this under their own terms. We do not send them your email address or your password. We do not share your information with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

Your account and profile are kept while your account exists. Coach chat history is kept so the conversation survives a refresh — you can wipe it yourself at any time using the clear button in the chat. If you delete your account, everything above is deleted.

Deleting your data

You can clear your coach conversation from inside the chat at any time. To delete your whole account and everything attached to it, use the delete option in your profile, or email us and we will do it.

Parents and guardians

MindZone asks every athlete to confirm that a parent or guardian has approved their use of the app before a profile is created. If you are a parent or guardian and you want to see what we hold about your child, or want it deleted, contact us and we will help.

If something serious is going on

MindZone is a training app. It is not therapy, and the coach is an AI — not a doctor, therapist, or counselor. Please talk to a trusted adult. These people are available any time, free:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line — text HOME to 741741
  • Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline — 1-800-422-4453
  • If someone is in danger right now, call 911.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change the date at the top.
